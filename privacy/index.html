2. What personal data we collect

We collect only the data you choose to send us through the website:

  • Newsletter opt-in (homepage toast and optional checkbox on the submit page): email address. Stored in Resend Contacts / Segments. During a transitional period the same email may also be submitted to Formspree form ID mlglprvj as a backup channel that is being phased out.
  • Mix / Event submission (forms on /submit/): artist or contact name, email, location, place of birth, birthday, Instagram / social handle, track pick, mix and photo links, story answers, quiz answers, consent checkbox values, and a honeypot field used only to detect bots. This data is processed by our Cloudflare Worker and sent in parallel to Resend (transactional email to us and a thank-you email to you) and Telegram (admin notification containing the submitted fields).
  • Ask a Question (homepage form): name (optional), Instagram (optional), question text, and technical form metadata (section, source). Submitted to Formspree form ID mkoqabob. This form does not collect an email address.

We do not run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or Cloudflare Web Analytics on this site. YouTube and Instagram content is linked externally (target="_blank"); we do not embed third-party players that set cookies on page load.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Newsletter: to send updates about episodes, studio sessions, and related news. Legal basis: consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The newsletter checkbox is unchecked by default; joining via the toast requires an active submit action.
  • Mix / Event submissions: to review submissions, communicate about possible publication or collaboration, and operate the Decks&Stories platform. Legal basis: steps prior to a contract / legitimate interest in evaluating artistic submissions and running the collective (Art. 6(1)(b) and/or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Ask a Question: to consider community questions for future video content. Legal basis: consent through voluntary participation (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

You may withdraw newsletter consent at any time via the unsubscribe link in our emails or by contacting us.

5. Retention

  • Newsletter: we keep your email while you remain subscribed. After you unsubscribe (or ask us to delete your contact), we remove you from the active mailing list and delete the contact record within 1 month, unless a longer period is required to demonstrate compliance with your unsubscribe request.
  • Mix / Event submissions: submission data processed via our Worker, Resend (transactional emails), and Telegram admin notifications is retained for up to 24 months from the submission date, then deleted or anonymised, unless we still need it for an ongoing collaboration with you or to establish / exercise / defend legal claims.

You can request earlier deletion at decksandstories@gmail.com.
